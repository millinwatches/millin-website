--------------------------------------------------
01 — CORE CREATIVE PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------

The digital experience must not feel like a website.

It must feel like a House.

The interface is architecture.

Navigation is movement through the House.

Typography is the voice of the House.

Photography is memory.

Video is atmosphere.

Whitespace is silence.

Craftsmanship is evidence.

Human presence is emotion.

The creation is the culmination.

The fundamental hierarchy is:

HERO SPACE
→ HERO CRAFT
→ HERO HUMAN
→ HERO CREATION
→ HERO COLLECTOR
→ HERO LEGACY

Never reverse this hierarchy.

The product must never become the starting point of the experience.

--------------------------------------------------
02 — DIGITAL MAISON DESIGN LANGUAGE
--------------------------------------------------

The visual language must combine:

European Maison
Contemporary museum
Private collector's residence
Master atelier
Editorial art book
Architectural photography
Horological culture

The overall feeling:

quiet

Avoid:

generic Swiss watch websites

excessive decoration

The result must feel like a living cultural institution.
