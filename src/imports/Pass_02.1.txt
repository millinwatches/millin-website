--------------------------------------------------

Replace the current slideshow-like opening with a true cinematic prologue.

The Prologue must feel like an opening film sequence.

Do not begin with complete watches.

Do not begin with product photography.

Do not begin with a collection.

Begin with fragments.

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

BLACK

↓

HOUSE OF MILLIN

The sequence should feel like:

something is being created.

something requires patience.

something is being preserved.

Recommended duration:

15–25 seconds.

Use:

slow crossfade
micro movement
subtle camera movement
very shallow depth of field
natural light
dark transitions

Avoid:

fast montage
commercial advertising cuts
dramatic zoom
excessive cinematic effects
CGI-looking luxury visuals

The Prologue should feel documentary.

The visitor should not yet know exactly what they are looking at.

Curiosity comes before explanation.
