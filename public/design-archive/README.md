## 🎨 DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

### The House is the Subject
Not the watch. The visitor experiences:

```
HOUSE → PHILOSOPHY → SPACE → ATELIER → 
CRAFT → HUMAN → COLLECTOR → BESPOKE → 
CREATION → PRIVATE APPOINTMENT
```

### Visual Constitution
- **Materials**: Limestone, walnut, stone, gold, enamel, engraved steel
- **Light**: European natural daylight (never artificial)
- **People**: Authentic presence, never performative
- **Craft**: Physical traces of human hands

### Luxury Through Restraint
- Negative space deliberate
- No excessive decoration
- No product-grid thinking
- Confident, private, quiet
