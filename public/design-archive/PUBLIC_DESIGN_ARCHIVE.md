## 🎨 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### **Visual Constitution**

All photography belongs to the MILLIN ORIGINAL visual world:

**Primary Materials**:
- Limestone
- Dark walnut
- Dark wood architectural panels
- Natural stone
- Muted gold
- Deep black
- Natural skin tones
- Natural metal
- Enamel
- Engraved steel
- Paper, wood, leather

**Light Philosophy**:
- Natural European daylight dominant
- Daylight leads always
- Architectural lamps support (never dominate)
- NO artificial luxury lighting
- NO excessive warm orange tones
- NO studio/fashion/commercial lighting

**Result**: Naturally photographed, not artificially rendered

---

### **Architectural Continuity**

All MASTER images derive from one physical House:

✓ French windows  
✓ Dark wooden frames  
✓ Formal garden  
✓ Limestone  
✓ Dark wood  
✓ Consistent mouldings and proportions  
✓ Consistent European architectural vocabulary  

ATELIER-ARC-002 is the approved architectural reference.

---

### **Human Continuity**

Artisan presence requirements:

✓ Authentic  
✓ Quiet, absorbed  
✓ Non-performative  
✓ Natural presence  

Never: camera-facing, posed, performing luxury, artificial smile, salesperson behavior

---

### **Craft Authenticity**

Every craft image communicates:

- MATERIAL (what is being worked)
- PRESSURE (human force applied)
- TIME (patient process)
- TRANSFORMATION (irreversible change)
- HUMAN TRACE (mark left by hand)

No CGI, plastic, fantasy colours, or exaggerated saturation.

**The craft must feel irreversible. A human hand left this mark.**

## 🎯 DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

### **The House is the Subject**

NOT the watch.

The visitor experiences:

```
HOUSE → PHILOSOPHY → SPACE → ATELIER → 
CRAFT → HUMAN → COLLECTOR → BESPOKE → 
CREATION → PRIVATE APPOINTMENT
```

The finished watch is a **consequence** of everything experienced before.

---

### **Luxury Through Restraint**

✓ Negative space deliberate  
✓ No excessive cards, borders, text  
✓ No decorative luxury effects  
✓ No excessive gold UI  
✓ No generic luxury imagery  

**Luxury is communicated through restraint, not decoration.**
